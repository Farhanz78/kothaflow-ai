# Commercial model

## Positioning

Sell "never miss a revenue/support call" and "automate repetitive phone work", not API minutes.

## Suggested Bangladesh launch tiers

These are starting hypotheses and should be validated with 10-20 design-partner businesses before final pricing.

| Plan | Monthly | Included AI minutes | Good fit |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Launch | BDT 4,990 | 300 | salons, small clinics, restaurants, local services |
| Growth | BDT 14,990 | 1,200 | e-commerce, multi-staff services, support lines |
| Scale | Custom | custom | high-volume BPO, enterprise, international |

Add setup/integration fees for custom CRMs, order systems, or complex workflows. Bill overage above included minutes with enough margin to cover telephony + model + orchestration + support + taxes.

## Unit-economics rule

Track **provider cost per completed business outcome**, not only cost/minute. A more expensive voice stack can be cheaper overall if it resolves faster and avoids human escalation.

Target healthy gross margin after carrier/model/orchestration costs. Never promise a margin before measuring real local SIP and voice-provider costs.
