# Glossary

**Agent** — configured AI phone worker.

**Barge-in** — caller speaks while AI is talking; AI should stop and listen.

**Containment / resolution** — acceptable call outcome without human transfer.

**Handoff / transfer** — route caller to a human.

**IPTSP** — internet protocol telephony service provider.

**Knowledge source** — approved tenant content used to ground answers.

**Provider adapter** — isolated voice vendor integration.

**RLS** — PostgreSQL row-level security.

**SIP** — Session Initiation Protocol for telephony routing.

**Tool** — approved business query/action such as calendar booking.

**Turn detection** — deciding when caller finished speaking.

**Voice eval** — repeatable conversation test scored for success, accuracy, latency, language, and handoff.
