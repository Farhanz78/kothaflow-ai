# Privacy and compliance checklist

Engineering/product checklist, not legal advice.

Before commercial launch:
- obtain current guidance on Bangladesh BTRC Call Center/BPO requirements for the actual model
- define legal entity and data locations
- publish privacy/data-processing terms
- configure AI disclosure and recording consent by jurisdiction
- maintain subprocessors/provider list
- establish incident/deletion processes

Data may include caller number, transcript, recording, appointment/order info, tool outputs, metadata, intent/sentiment/QA labels.

Per-tenant controls should include recording, disclosure, retention, access roles, export/delete workflow, outbound consent policy.

Do not store data merely because a provider exposes it.
